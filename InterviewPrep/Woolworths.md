# Job Description

Technology Expertise: Very strong and current background (10+ years) in developing,deploying, and managing large volume .NET services. Strong knowledge also required in:   
o Event Based Architecture   
o Domain Driven Design   
o Microsoft Azure   
o Github   
o AB Testing   
o TDD   
o Cloud Architecture   
● Leadership Experience: Proven team leadership experience, with a minimum of five 
(5) years successfully running and mentoring development teams. A background in 
forming and developing teams is highly desirable for this role. Our teams operate 
under SCRUM methodologies, experience as a SCRUM master is preferred.   
● E-commerce Focus: Experience building and optimising mobile applications 
specifically for large-scale, enterprise-level e-commerce platforms or transactional 
based platforms.   
● Problem-Solving Prowess: Exceptional analytical and problem-solving capabilities, 
with a track record of diagnosing technical challenges/defects effectively.   
● Work Style: A demonstrated hands-on approach to leadership and development, 
driving both strategic oversight and direct code contributions. A high degree of 
autonomy and strong time management skills are essential for self-organisation in 
this role.    
● Communication & Timezones: Excellent communication and collaboration skills with 
a proven ability to work effectively across geographically distributed teams and 
multiple timezones. Proven experience communicating with business stakeholders 
internal and external is a must
